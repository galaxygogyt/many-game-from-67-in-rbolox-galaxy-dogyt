``` ### Summary of New Additions: 1. **2-Minute Match Timer**: Placed prominently in the top-center HUD counting down from `02:00`. 2. **Dynamic Random Events**: Triggered automatically every 15–20 seconds: - **殭屍爆發 (Zombie Outbreak)**: Hordes of green zombies swarm tanks and deal melee damage. - **隕石雨 (Meteor Shower)**: Warning target circles appear on screen followed by explosive meteors causing AOE splash damage. - **空投補給 (Air Drop Supply)**: Health (+50 HP) or Ammo Magazine crates land on the battlefield. - **沙塵暴 (Sandstorm)**: Lowers visibility and slows all tank movements. - **EMP 電磁脈衝 (EMP Blast)**: Temporarily disables shooting for 5 seconds. 3. **Sound & Visual Upgrades**: Added zombie groans, event alert chimes, screen shake, floating damage text numbers, and event banners.
